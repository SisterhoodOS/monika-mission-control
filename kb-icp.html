The Growth-Stage Builder

Real traction, broken infrastructure

Who They Are

A founder who has built something genuinely real in a market where that took serious grit. They have customers, revenue, a team of some kind. But the business runs on their personal energy, judgment, and relationships — not on systems. They are growing, and the growth is starting to expose the gaps.

The Strength

They have already proven something works. Market fit exists. The product or service has real demand.

The Gap

Operational infrastructure hasn't kept pace with the growth. Processes live in the founder's head. Delegation fails because there's nothing to hand over.

What They Say on the Call

  • "I can't step away because everything runs through me."
  • "We've been growing but it feels messier, not cleaner."
  • "I need to hire but I don't know who because I haven't written the job down."
  • "I keep saying I'll build that system eventually."

What They Actually Need

An operational architecture that lets the business run without being personally held by the founder. Documented processes, clear ownership, a priority sequence for what to build first.

Best Entry Point

3-Hour Mind Mapping ($397 special). Enough depth to map the real picture. If the session reveals significant scope, position DFY ($15K) as the path forward.

Qualifying Signal

Listen for "eventually." If they say it three or more times in discovery, the business is stuck at exactly those points. That is where the work is.
"I don't doubt you can figure it out yourself. The question is what it costs you in time and energy to figure it out alone, and whether that is the best use of what you've got right now."

The Visionary Innovator

Wide scope, early execution

Who They Are

A founder building something with genuine ambition — often in climate, infrastructure, technology, or a multi-vertical portfolio. The vision is large and real. The company is early (2–10 people). Execution is still being built around the vision, not yet driving it.

This founder often works across multiple sub-brands or verticals. The strategic priority sequence is unclear. Partners and investors get the vision but struggle to see the executable near-term story.

The Strength

The vision is bold and the thinking is non-incremental. This is not a person solving a small problem.

The Gap

The vision expands faster than the execution. The operational story is not yet legible to external partners. No clear "what is real right now" layer.

What They Say on the Call

  • "We're working on several things at once — climate, fintech, telecom, AI."
  • "Investors keep asking what the near-term revenue looks like and I'm still building the answer."
  • "I have people but I'm still doing most of the important things myself."
  • "The concept is solid, I just need help on the business architecture side."

What They Actually Need

A priority sequence. A clear distinction between what is real now and what is roadmap. An operational story that makes serious partners say yes. Often a strong AI Implementation fit because technology is core to their product vision.

Best Entry Point

Free 30-min Audit to establish trust and map the real constraint. AI Implementation is often the highest-value offer for this profile — lead with it once the technical gap is confirmed in discovery.

Qualifying Signal

Portfolio breadth is the tell. If they are working across 3+ verticals with a small team, the operational architecture is almost certainly missing. The vision is real. The structure isn't there yet.
"The most valuable thing I can do for you is help you see your own situation with clarity. You have enormous vision. What you need is someone who respects that AND asks the grounded questions nobody else is asking you."

03

The Independent Expert

Deep expertise, missing the infrastructure

Who They Are

A highly experienced professional — investor, consultant, specialist, creative director — who has 10–20 years of genuine domain expertise and a strong network, but no unified vehicle to leverage it at scale. Often currently employed or working independently without formal structure.

They are moving toward building their own thing, or have already started but it's still running through their personal relationships and energy rather than a system.

The Strength

Deep expertise, real track record, global network. The raw materials for a leveraged independent practice are already there.

The Gap

No legibility. The expertise isn't packaged. The network works through personal relationships, not infrastructure. There's no vehicle that scales without their energy.

What They Say on the Call

  • "I have the experience and the network but I haven't quite built the vehicle yet."
  • "If the right person found me tomorrow they'd have to ask me to explain everything."
  • "I know what I want to build, I just haven't built the infrastructure around it."
  • "My network does the work for me — for now."

What They Actually Need

Positioning that makes the expertise legible. A system that lets the network work for them instead of through them. A vehicle — a brand, a practice, a methodology — that exists independent of their personal capacity.

Best Entry Point

Free 30-min Audit framed as mapping the independent practice. Come as a peer. Do not pitch. Let the conversation show what is possible. Often becomes a referral/partnership relationship even when not yet ready to buy.

Qualifying Signal

The "for now" tells you everything. If they are relying on their network "for now," the urgency isn't visible to them yet. Frame the risk of waiting, not the reward of acting. That lands differently for this profile.
"What you have — the background, the network, the track record — is genuinely rare. What's missing is legibility. The way it's held together in your memory and relationships means it can only ever be as big as your personal capacity."
