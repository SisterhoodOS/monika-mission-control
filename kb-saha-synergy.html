Business Clarity Sessions

BCS Offer

Business Clarity Sessions (BCS) is an AI-powered business audit and strategy service. It is positioned as a fast, affordable entry point for founders and entrepreneurs who need clarity on their business model, positioning, or growth levers. The format is lightweight by design, keeping Sophia's time commitment low while delivering high perceived value.

Standard BCS (90 Minutes)

  • Session: 90-minute AI-powered business audit call
  • Deliverable: Written strategy report delivered within 48 hours of the session
  • Follow-up: 7-day WhatsApp support window after delivery

Premium BCS (3 Hours)

  • Session: Extended 3-hour deep-dive (Strategic Mind Mapping format)
  • Deliverable: Comprehensive written strategy document within 48 hours
  • Follow-up: 7-day WhatsApp support window
  • Price: $997 regular / $397 April-June 2026 special (Saha Synergy launch offer)

Backend & Tech Stack

The BCS booking flow is designed to run fully automated after a single afternoon of setup. The flow removes manual steps between payment and booking confirmation.

Booking Flow (End-to-End)

  1. Client arrives via ad, social post, WhatsApp referral, or direct link
  2. Lands on BCS landing page
  3. Clicks to pay via Stripe (payment processed, funds routed to Currenxie)
  4. Stripe triggers automated email with Calendly booking link
  5. Client selects session time in Calendly
  6. Sophia receives automated notification of confirmed booking
  7. Client receives confirmation email with session prep instructions

BCS Marketing Playbook

BCS marketing is direct and community-driven. The primary channels are Bali and Canggu-based group chats and warm networks, supplemented by Luma masterclass events and Meta Ads targeting entrepreneurs.

Primary Channels

WhatsApp Group Outreach

Sophia and Helena reach out directly in Bali and Canggu group chats. Target communities include Bali digital nomad groups, yoga and wellness communities, and Canggu entrepreneur circles.

  • Bali digital nomad groups
  • Yoga / wellness communities
  • Canggu community and entrepreneur groups
  • Helena's personal network (approximately 600-woman audience)

Voice Note Strategy

Personalized voice note outreach via WhatsApp. Voice notes outperform text in conversion and trust-building. Sophia or Helena records a short voice note introducing BCS to warm contacts in relevant groups. Feels personal, bypasses message fatigue.

Luma Masterclass Events

Free or low-cost Luma events used as top-of-funnel lead generation. Masterclass attendees are warm leads who have already sampled Sophia's facilitation style. BCS is pitched as a natural next step for founders who want personalized strategy.

Meta Ads

Paid traffic targeting entrepreneurs, coaches, and founders in relevant demographics. Campaigns emphasize the quick ROI and clarity outcome of a single session. Target: women in Bali, Southeast Asia, and international digital nomad hubs who identify as entrepreneurs.
