Decision Audit
Supporter Code · Menstrual Phase · Venus/Venus Dasha (ending May 26)
The Supporter's decision-making challenge: she can feel so attuned to what others need that she loses the thread of her own signal. The audit below helps her locate her own yes/no before she extends it to anyone else.
The Supporter Decision Filter
1. Where is this coming from?
Is this impulse coming from overflow (genuine desire, real capacity, full cup), or from fear (fear of not being needed, fear of disappointing, fear of being dispensable)? Full cup gives. Empty cup depletes and eventually resents.
2. Have I given myself the same thing?
The Supporter shadow is the caretaker who never receives. Before extending support to anyone, check: have I received this from myself or another? Can I give it without needing it back from them?
3. Am I visible in this?
Ketu in H1 creates invisible-izing patterns, making herself small in her own story. Before any significant decision, check: is Monika present in this outcome, or has she designed herself out of it?
4. Does this pass the far-sight test?
Gate 17: six months from now, does this decision still make sense? She has the capacity to see consequences before they arrive. Ask the far-sighted self, not just the present self.
Phase Check: Winter Decision Protocol
