Ideal Client Profile

The 7 Avatars

SPC speaks to seven distinct women. Each avatar names a different flavor of the same core wound: she is too big for the container she has been living in.

Avatar 01
Corporate Ceiling Woman

Six-figure salary, glass office, the respect of her peers — and she is done. The system has given her everything it promised and it still does not feel like enough. She is not burned out. She is outgrown.

Avatar 02
Leap-Taker Who's Struggling

She already left corporate. The income is inconsistent, the clients are sometimes wrong, and the inner foundation she thought she had is shakier than she expected. She needs roots, not more strategy.

Avatar 03
Silent Caretaker

She holds everyone. The team, the family, the household. The slow quiet erosion has taken years. She has a vision she cannot say out loud because saying it feels like a betrayal. She is ready to be seen.

Avatar 04
Southeast Asian Achiever

Drawn to Sophia's cultural context and lived experience in this region. The IDR payment option matters — it signals that this program sees her. High achiever, high expectations, deeply loyal once trust is built.

Avatar 05
Gifted Undercharger

Genuinely talented. Has the gift, has the results, has the testimonials. Still undercharges, over-delivers, and apologizes for her prices. She does not need more mindset content. She needs somatic work.

Avatar 06
Sophisticated Stuckness Woman

Knows all the theory. Has read the books, attended the retreats, done the certifications. Still in the same holding pattern since 2022. She does not need more insight. She needs activation and accountability.

Avatar 07
Threshold Woman

Something ended. A marriage, a career, an identity she wore for a decade. She is in the in-between — nothing fully composted, nothing new yet planted. She knows this is a threshold. She needs someone to walk it with her.

Pipeline Framework

The 6 A's

A structured sales pipeline mapped to the psychological journey of the buyer. Each stage has a defined trigger, deliverable, and handoff point.

A1
Attention

Content hooks, lead magnets, and the Soul Path Quiz bring her into the ecosystem. She finds Sisterhood OS because something she saw reflected her truth.

A2
Activation

The masterclass structure and landing page convert passive interest into committed attendance. She registers, shows up, and has a real experience in the room.

A3
Application

Airtable auto-sorts submissions by fit level. Green = book a call immediately. Yellow = manual review required. Red = graceful decline with a warm message and redirect.

A4
Assessment

15-minute qualifying call. Four gap areas explored: identity, belonging, purpose, and support. Helena or Sophia listens for readiness and fit — not just desire.

A5
Assurance

She needs to know this is real. Deliverables: 12-page product guide PDF, detailed FAQ, case studies, comparison chart, walkthrough video. Remove doubt, build trust.

A6
Agreement

Stripe link sent. On payment, Zapier fires: Airtable tags updated, welcome sequence triggered, Circle community invite sent, orientation materials delivered. The machine handles the moment, the team handles the human.

Helena's Top-Performing Hooks

From @helena.seramor analysis — hooks that have demonstrated viral reach and avatar resonance. Study the pattern: simple, true, slightly uncomfortable.

"Being a woman who provides for herself is a privilege."

608,000 views

"Sometimes I'm not available for anyone."

309,000 views

"All that worry and we all end up in the same place."

265,000 views

Hook Pattern Notes

  • Each hook is one sentence. No setup needed.
  • All three speak a truth she has felt but not said. The content validates an unspoken interior experience.
  • None are negative or fear-based. They are observational and slightly sovereign.
  • The privilege framing works because it reframes something assumed to be basic as actually rare and earned.
  • The availability hook works because it names the thing women feel guilty for wanting.
  • The worry hook works because it dissolves the mental load with a grounding truth.
